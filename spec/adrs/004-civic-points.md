# ADR-004 — Civic Points as Useful-Work Incentive

## Decision

Civic Points reward verifiable useful connectivity contribution.

Points are not a protocol settlement currency.

Eligible benefits include:
- priority;
- queue preference;
- sponsored access;
- fee reductions;
- community perks.

Money redemption may exist through a separately governed settlement program.

## Evidence

Points are issued only after replay-safe, authenticated contribution evidence.

## Anti-gaming

The system rejects self-generated loops, duplicate receipts, replay, circular traffic and unacknowledged service.

## Consequence

Economics cannot become a source of ungrounded "mining" activity.
